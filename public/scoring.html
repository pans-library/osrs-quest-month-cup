Points by place

Checks

Rules most groups would want a scheme to follow. A cross means the scheme breaks the rule for this season.

Matchups

Two players with fixed results over the season. A tick or cross means the scheme agrees or disagrees with the expected winner. A question mark is a judgement call: there's no right answer, but it shows which style each scheme rewards.

Shape

Share to 1st is how much of a quest's points go to the winner. 1st ÷ 2nd is how much a win is worth over runner-up. Swing is the most one quest can change a gap between two players.
